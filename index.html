Method & honesty

What the number means — and what it does not.

01

The first namaz

Jibril عليه السلام led the Prophet ﷺ at the Ka‘bah over two days, beginning with Dhuhr after zawal, then Asr, Maghrib, Isha and Fajr, repeated at the end times: “the time is between these two.” This chronology is reported from Ibn ‘Abbas in Abu Dawud.

02

The date is a convention

27 Rajab is commemorated, but no single agreed calendar day for Isra’ wal-Mi‘raj was transmitted. Historians place it roughly 1–1.5 years before Hijrah. Mihrab therefore locks 27 February 621 CE as an explicit convention, not a certified date. Moving within the scholarly window changes roughly one thousand slots in a total above 2.5 million.

03

Leap years, exactly

The engine walks real UTC calendar days. It never multiplies a rounded year by 365. Hijri years are shown only as context because prayer slots follow the solar day.

04

Age 13 is an average

Farz begins at physical puberty. Fifteen lunar years is a classical fallback; use the real date when known. These controls are transparent estimates and notes, not a fatwa.
